I'm Vincent, an 18 years old cybersecurity student based in Bordeaux. I first got into cybersecurity in 2022, and started taking it seriously in 2024 when I began studying at Bordeaux Ynov Campus.

I was part of **ECSC Team France 2025 🇫🇷** and I'm a member of **Ynov Cyber Crew 🗡️** for the 2025-2026 academic year.

A lot of that learning came through CTFs. I started out with *reverse engineering* and *binary exploitation*, but over time I found myself
getting more and more drawn to *web exploitation* and *pentesting*. I love the mix of creativity, technical depth, and problem-solving, and I
try to write the kind of writeups I would have wanted to read when I was starting out.

Right now, I'm especially interested in *Windows and Active Directory* environments, because they feel very different from classic Linux
exploitation and often reward a more practical way of thinking.

Beyond CTFs, I also like digging into real-world applications and turning that curiosity into actual research. I recently worked on **CVE-
2026-34036**, which pushed me to look at targets less like puzzles and more like real systems.

This blog is mostly where I keep track of what I learn, what I find interesting, and the techniques I don't want to forget later.
