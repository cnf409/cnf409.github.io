I'm Vincent, a 19 year old cybersecurity student based in Bordeaux, now in my third year (B3 Cyber) at Ynov Campus. I first got into cybersecurity in 2022, and started taking it seriously in 2024 when I began studying at Bordeaux Ynov Campus.

I was part of **ECSC Team France 🇫🇷** in 2025 and again in 2026, and I'm a member of **Ynov Cyber Crew 🗡️** for the 2026-2027 academic year.

A lot of that learning came through CTFs. I started out with *reverse engineering* and *binary exploitation*, but over time I found myself
getting more and more drawn to *web exploitation* and *pentesting*. I love the mix of creativity, technical depth, and problem-solving, and I
try to write the kind of writeups I would have wanted to read when I was starting out.

Over the past year I spent a lot of time on Hack The Box machines, and *Windows and Active Directory* pentesting went from something I was
curious about to something I'm comfortable with. These days my focus is on *web vulnerability research*: finding bugs in real applications
and looking for new exploitation techniques instead of replaying the known ones.

Beyond CTFs, I also like digging into real-world applications and turning that curiosity into actual research. I recently worked on
**CVE-2026-34036**, which pushed me to look at targets less like puzzles and more like real systems.

This blog is where I put the writeups worth reading, my personal research, and my opinions on whatever I think deserves one.
