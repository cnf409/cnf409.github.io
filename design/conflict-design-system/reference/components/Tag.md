Tag pills: mono `#tag` in an ink frame; links fill with dense screentone on hover.

- `<a class="tag">#php</a>` in a `ul.tags`. Add `.tag__count` for counts and `aria-current="page"` for the current tag (ink fill).
- Inside cards, use `span.tag` in `ul.tags.tags--sm` (not links, because the whole card is the link).
- Up to 9 on a writeup, 7 on a list row, 4 on a pinned card.
